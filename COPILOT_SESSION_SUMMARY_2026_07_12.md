## Core Discovery: Mitzi's Operating System

### The Three-Thing Conversation
Mitzi works at the intersection of:
- **Leadership** — Who am I? Am I leading from integrity and authenticity?
- **Thinking** — How am I thinking? Where is my attention? What are the tradeoffs?
- **Technology** — Are my tools in service of the outcomes, or undermining them?

These aren't separate subjects. They're one conversation.

### The Core Philosophy
- Leaders today ignite thinking in others, not command
- The job is to **surface what's invisible**
- Tools should help people *see* more clearly, not *think for them*
- Every tool must answer: "Are we using this in a way that serves the outcome?"

### What "Surfacing the Invisible" Means
- Behaviors in meetings (MII)
- Where attention is placed (OII, Horizons)
- Time horizons and tradeoffs (OII, Horizons)
- The gap between intention and action (all tools)

### The Tone
- Never prescriptive
- Never "I have the answer"
- Always "Here's a way to see this more clearly"
- Careful, reflective, grounded in frameworks but deeply practical
- Honors the complexity and emotional labour

### The Line We Don't Cross
- We don't automate away the leader's thinking
- We don't use technology to undermine outcomes
- We're modeling responsible, intentional use of tools (including AI)

## The "Simulated Empathy and AI" Workshop Insight

**The Real Tension:**
A doctor was getting inadequate care from her physician (due to system depletion). She turned to ChatGPT. The AI simulated empathy and she felt heard. It worked — she felt better.

**The Question:** *"Does it matter if it's simulated empathy if the outcome is relief and better thinking?"*

**The Deeper Question:** *"The question is whether we use AI to extract more from an already depleted system or to restore something we've been losing."*

**What This Means:**
- Mitzi's tools aren't just diagnostics
- They're **resistance tools** — asking leaders to slow down, notice depletion, choose presence over simulation
- They help leaders **notice what they're losing** and choose differently

### The Workshop Should Surface:
1. Where am I replacing presence with simulation?
2. Where is the system depleting people?
3. What safeguards do I need?
4. What could we restore instead?

## The Workflow Decision (Provisional)

**Mitzi will:**
- Continue thinking in Claude (proposals, content, philosophy, thinking work)
- Use GitHub/Copilot for *building* (when she wants to create/deploy tools)

**How it works:**
- Mitzi tells Copilot "I'm working on [project]"
- Copilot reads CONTEXT.md + CLAUDE.md from that repo
- Copilot understands the context without re-explaining
- At end of session: auto-commit with update to CONTEXT.md
- Next session: seamless pickup

**For iteration on websites:**
- Mitzi describes a change
- Copilot makes the change and pushes to repo
- Netlify auto-deploys (~30 seconds)
- Mitzi refreshes browser to see it live
- Mitzi gives feedback; repeat

**Visual feedback:**
- If Mitzi takes a screenshot and pastes it, Copilot can see it and understand what she's describing
- Copilot can read video transcripts (Mitzi pastes them)

# Homepage review — second opinion brief

*A quick orientation for anyone reviewing the current site and the new homepage draft
side by side. Written 22 August 2026.*

---

## What's changing, and why

Mitzi Wyman's site (mitziwyman.com) is being rethought — starting with the homepage.
The goal is **not** to make it sound like a more polished, conventional
executive-coaching or consultancy site. The goal is to make it more distinctively
*Mitzi* — her actual territory is the quality of thinking when decisions matter, at
the intersection of leadership, thinking and technology, not "leadership" as a generic
category.

We're borrowing **structure and level of proof** from a comparable practitioner's site
(named organisations, credentials, testimonials, clear routes to work together) —
without borrowing their language or positioning. Generic phrases like "navigate
complexity" or "unlock potential" are being deliberately avoided.

## Where to look

- **Live site:** mitziwyman.com — this is what's public today, and predates this
  redesign work.
- **Draft homepage:** https://6a8a07f79d31fa0fb070b205--jocular-salmiakki-17943d.netlify.app/homepage-draft.html
  — only the homepage has been redesigned so far. Inner pages (About, Courses,
  Organisations) are still the old site for now, so don't judge those against the new
  direction yet.

**Known issue on the draft:** several nav links don't go anywhere yet — About
redirects straight back to the homepage, and Technology and Contact don't exist as
pages at all. Navigation generally still needs work. Not a design question, just
flagging so it doesn't get mistaken for a design decision.

## The direction, in brief

- Keep the existing visual world: the video hero, the current colour palette
  (unchanged deliberately), serif editorial headings, generous white space.
- Keep "Leadership. Thinking. Technology." as a genuine three-part structure — Mitzi's
  own video already frames this as "the intersection," and all three stay equal
  weight rather than one being elevated over the others.
- A distinctive point to land clearly: Mitzi doesn't only think and write about
  leadership and technology — **she builds the tools herself** (the diagnostics, the
  apps). That's unusual in this space and worth making explicit rather than implied.
- Testimonials and named organisations should be visible on the homepage, not buried
  on inner pages — but curated, not a wall of logos.
- "Fresh Thinking" — Mitzi's ongoing, evolving space for ideas still in motion — stays
  as a real destination on the site, not just a link out.

## Proposed shape of the new homepage

Five fluid movements rather than seven labelled sections:

1. **Hero** — the video, plus a sharper opening line than the current placeholder.
2. **The territory** — a short paragraph on how Mitzi actually works, including the
   "I build the tools too" point.
3. **Leadership · Thinking · Technology** — three real, equal cards, each with its own
   distinct hook (Technology's is "what if you could see what's happening to thinking
   in a room?").
4. **Proof** — a small number of testimonials, chosen for what they reveal about the
   work (not generic praise), plus named organisations.
5. **Ways in, then Fresh Thinking** — routes to work together, closing with an
   invitation into the ongoing ideas space.

## What to keep from the current live site

Not everything on the existing site is being replaced — some of it is already strong
and just needs surfacing better rather than rewriting. The clearest example: the live
site's tools page (mitziwyman.com/suite) already has well-developed descriptions of
the Meeting Intelligence Index, the Organisational Intelligence Index, and the
companion tools — considerably more developed than the placeholder Technology card on
the new draft. That existing copy is a strong candidate to become the substance of the
new Technology page, rather than writing it from scratch.

Worth reviewing the current site with that question in mind: what else already works
and just needs a better home in the new structure, rather than being rewritten?

## Where a second opinion would help most

- Does the three-part "Leadership · Thinking · Technology" structure read as one
  coherent territory, or still as three separate services?
- Does "I build the tools too" land as distinctive, without sounding like a tech-sales
  pitch?
- Testimonial strategy — curated few vs. showing the breadth of names.
- Whether the "Fresh Thinking" destination makes sense as pitched, or needs a clearer
  description of what it actually contains.
- What else on the current live site is strong enough to carry forward as-is, rather
  than being rewritten?

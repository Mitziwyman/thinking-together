A few minutes, turned inward

Most of us are far better at looking after other people than ourselves. Attention flows outward almost without our noticing — to the people we're responsible for, the work in front of us, the next thing being asked of us. It's easier to focus on the outside than on what we actually need.

If you knew your own health mattered as much as helping everyone else — how would that change what you do today, and what would your next step be?

Health means different things to different people, and different things on different days. It might be physical, mental, emotional, or spiritual. You choose which part to bring into focus — and you can come back another time and hold a different one in mind.

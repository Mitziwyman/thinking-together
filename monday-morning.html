A few minutes before the week takes hold

Monday morning has its own pull. The week arrives already full — inboxes, meetings, other people's priorities — and it's easy to be three things deep before you've decided any of them for yourself.

Five minutes before that happens gives you a chance to set the week on your own terms. What you're actually bringing to it. What deserves your attention, and what you can let go of to give it that attention properly.

It doesn't take the week's demands away. But it means you meet Friday having chosen the shape of the week, rather than just having survived it.

My work sits at the intersection of three things I don't think of as separate.

Leadership

Who you are, what you bring into the room, and what leadership requires when the answers keep changing. I work with boards and leadership teams to build trust, transparency and intellectual honesty into how they actually decide.

Thinking

The conditions that let people think for themselves, listen well, challenge assumptions and exercise good judgement. This is Nancy Kline's Thinking Environment — the method behind everything I do.

Technology

Technology in the broad sense, not just AI. My tools make visible what's usually invisible in how meetings and decisions actually happen — extending the work of the room, not replacing it.

About Mitzi

I help boards and leadership teams see the thinking behind their decisions, not just the decisions themselves — because in a world that keeps changing faster than most governance was built for, trust, transparency and intellectual honesty in the room matter as much as the strategy in it. I trained on Nancy Kline's Time to Think faculty, which is where my method comes from. My fascination with technology and AI has led me to build tools that make that quality of thinking visible, rather than assumed.

I trained as a lawyer, with an LLM in International Law from UCL, and later took an MSc in Organisational Psychiatry and Psychology at King's College London — the point where my interest shifted from what people argue to how they actually think. Today I'm a coach, facilitator and teacher on Nancy Kline's Time to Think faculty, faculty of the NHS Leadership Capital Academy, and a facilitator with Windsor Leadership.

# Homepage rethink — working plan

*Consolidated from Mitzi's brief + follow-up comments during the 22 Aug 2026 session,
before any changes are made to `homepage-draft.html`. Written so this can be picked up
cold in a future session without re-reading the whole conversation.*

---

## The core intent (from Mitzi's original brief)

Make the site **more distinctively Mitzi, not more generically professional.**
Learn structure and level of proof from Jane Adshead-Grant's site — not her language
or positioning. Avoid all generic exec-coaching phrases (navigate complexity, unlock
potential, transformational leadership, etc.).

The territory isn't "leadership" alone — it's **the quality of thinking when decisions
matter**, at the intersection of leadership, thinking, judgement, attention,
conversations, meetings, governance and technology.

## What's confirmed and settled

- **Palette: unchanged.** Current slate/teal/off-white stays exactly as is. Colour and
  movement come from the video and photography, not the shell.
- **Visual world: unchanged.** Serif editorial headings, clean typography, video hero,
  spacious layout, alternating sections — all kept.
- **"Leadership. Thinking. Technology." stays as a genuine three-part structure**, not
  collapsed or reordered. Mitzi's video already frames this as "the intersection," so
  the three stay equal-weight and woven together, not three separate service pillars.
- **New distinctiveness point to build in:** Mitzi doesn't just think and write about
  leadership and technology — she **builds the tools herself** (the diagnostics, the
  apps). This needs an explicit line, most likely inside the Technology section, e.g.
  (draft, still to react to): *"Most people who write about leadership stop at the
  framework. I build the tools too."*
- **Testimonial pool is now large and fully sourced.** Mitzi supplied a transcription
  file plus two more source documents (a Notes backup and the old Fulcrum flyer),
  which between them confirm 18 named, senior testimonials across two contexts —
  Thinking Environment / courses work, and Fulcrum dialogue circles. This resolves
  the Lyne/MaCapra gap completely (see full list below) and adds several new names:
  Professor Deborah Christie (Clinical Director, **UCLH** — matches Mitzi's confirmed
  organisation list), Kay Wren, Tim Doran (RNLI), Susi Clarke (NHS Primary Care
  Workforce Programme), Gemma Glanville (Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust), and
  Fleur McDonnell.
  **Open tension, not yet resolved:** the original brief was "two testimonials, not a
  wall." Having now seen the full range, Mitzi commented that the *breadth and
  seniority* of the names is itself powerful proof. This needs a conscious decision —
  see "Still open" below — rather than defaulting to either extreme.
- **Organisations to name** (confirmed directly by Mitzi, supersedes the earlier
  screenshot-derived list): Arup, Skanska, Health Innovation Network, Sony Pictures,
  UCLH, Princess Alexandra NHS Trust, NHS NLFT, Mazars, KPMG. No logo image files
  exist yet, so this will be a plain-text "worked with" line rather than a logo strip,
  unless Mitzi supplies logo artwork later.
- **Fresh Thinking stays**, but needs to become a real destination on this site rather
  than a link out to `thinking.mitziwyman.com` — there's a live redirect rule that may
  send that link straight back to the homepage, so as it stands it may not go anywhere.
  Logo returns to homepage, not to Fresh Thinking.
- **For You page: keep the page and its opening line** ("People who lead rarely get
  asked how things actually are") — it's already distinctive and shouldn't become a
  conventional About page. **The photo is being replaced** — the current photo
  (`hero-you.jpg`) has Mitzi looking at the other person, not the camera, which reads
  as off. Two candidates on the table:
  - `mitzi.jpg` / `mitzi-wyman.jpg` — existing photo, direct eye contact, already in
    the project.
  - A new AI-generated image (ChatGPT), saved as
    `~/Downloads/you-page-photo-candidate-coach-backshot.png` — Mitzi shown from
    behind (solves the "AI never gets my face right" problem) with a client shown

- **Courses page is also flagged as out of date** (dates/prices stale) — this is a

## Proposed homepage structure (five fluid movements, not seven labelled sections)

1. **Hero** — video stays. Headline under review: keep "Leadership. Thinking.
   Technology." as the identity tag, paired with a sharper first line that hasn't
   been agreed yet (a draft was offered — "The harder the decision, the easier it is
   to mistake activity for thinking" — not yet approved).
2. **The territory** — a short paragraph replacing the plain "What I offer" label,
   combining track record with how she actually works. Possibly where the "I build
   the tools too" line lands.
3. **Leadership · Thinking · Technology**, as three real, equal cards — Technology's
   copy gets the "what if you could see what's happening to thinking in a room?"
   hook, and should also carry the builder point.
4. **Proof** — two testimonials, early: Nancy Kline (full quote) + one NHS/governance
   voice (still being chosen). Organisation names appear here or nearby, as text.
5. **Ways in, then Fresh Thinking** — Courses and Organisations as the two routes to
   work together, then a closing invitation into Fresh Thinking as a real page.

  the homepage proof (e.g. a compact names-and-roles line alongside the two full

  avoid a wall. Needs a conscious middle path, not a default.

  include Buggins, Thornley, Christie (ties to UCLH), Wren, and the Fulcrum names for
  a more philosophical placement (About/Fresh Thinking, per the original brief's
  suggestion for Lyne specifically).
- **Confirm the three name/org spelling discrepancies** above before anything is
  published.
- **Nav sequencing**: build About and Technology as real pages in this same pass, or
  finish the homepage narrative first and do inner pages after? (Both are currently

- **Hero headline wording**: draft offered, not yet approved.
- **Exact placement of the "builder" line**: Technology card, territory paragraph, or
  both.

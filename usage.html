How the companion tools are being used

A quiet count of how many people have opened and completed each thinking tool. No names, no personal data — only anonymous tallies. This page reads the live figures each time you open it.

What the numbers mean. People is how many separate browsers have visited (the closest thing to unique visitors). Opened is how many times a tool was started. Completed is how many of those went all the way through to a reflection. Everything here is anonymous — the tracker never records who anyone is.

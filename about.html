About Mitzi

Lawyer by background, organisational psychologist by training, faculty for Nancy Kline's Time to Think.

What I do now

I help boards and leadership teams see the thinking behind their decisions, not just the decisions themselves — because in a world that keeps changing faster than most governance was built for, trust, transparency and intellectual honesty in the room matter as much as the strategy in it. That's the territory I work in: leadership, thinking and technology, held together rather than treated as three separate things.

From law to how people actually think

I trained as a lawyer, with an LLM in International Law from UCL, and later took an MSc in Organisational Psychiatry and Psychology at King's College London — the point where my interest shifted from what people argue to how they actually think. Along the way I was awarded a scholarship to study social enterprise at Cambridge.

Today I'm a coach, facilitator and teacher on Nancy Kline's Time to Think faculty, faculty of the NHS Leadership Capital Academy, and a facilitator with Windsor Leadership.

Working with difference

I'm Anglo-Japanese, and I've lived and been educated across several continents — a background that's taught me to work with difference rather than around it. My own practice is informed by a blend of Buddhist practice and Quaker wisdom.

The quality of a decision depends on the quality of the thinking that produced it.
